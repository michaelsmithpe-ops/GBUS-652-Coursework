Hi Lisa,

Thanks for the quick heads-up on PO-7795. Here is how we understand the two options for the 400 TR-29 tires:

- Option 1: 160 tires on the scheduled date and the remaining 240 two weeks later. On our current schedule, this leaves us no units short and causes no delay.
- Option 2: all 400 tires together, four days after the scheduled date. On our current schedule, this leaves us 40 units short and delays orders by 3 days.

3. Is there any other way to bring forward some or all of the 240 remaining tires?

Brookfield will confirm its choice within the two business days you asked for. Any answers on the questions above before then will help us decide.
