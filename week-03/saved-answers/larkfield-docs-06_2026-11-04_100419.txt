ANSWER
Based on the policy, for checks deposited at a branch, the first $275 of the day's check deposits is available on the next business day, with the remainder available on the second business day after the deposit [funds-availability-policy.html]. So regardless of the paycheck being $1,000, the member can use **$275** of it on the next business day.

One condition to note: this assumes the deposit was made before 5 p.m. on a business day. If it was deposited after 5 p.m., or on a weekend/holiday, it is treated as received the next business day, which would push back the "next business day" availability date by one day [funds-availability-policy.html].
