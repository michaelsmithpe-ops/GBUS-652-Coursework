ANSWER
Since the member reported the loss the same day (within two business days of learning about it), they pay no more than $50 of the loss from unauthorized transactions [card-dispute-procedure.html].

Note: this $50 cap applies to prompt loss/theft reporting. If unauthorized transactions later show up on a statement and the member fails to report those within 60 days after the statement was sent, they could become liable in full for transactions occurring after that 60-day window [card-dispute-procedure.html].
