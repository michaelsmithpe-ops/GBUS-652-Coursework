ANSWER
Rush delivery for a replacement debit card costs $30 [consumer-fee-schedule.html].

Note the exception: if the old card was reported lost or stolen because of fraud, the replacement card is free [consumer-fee-schedule.html] — the sources don't specify whether this fraud exception also waives the rush delivery charge itself, so if that situation applies, you may want to confirm separately whether rush delivery would still incur the $30 fee.
