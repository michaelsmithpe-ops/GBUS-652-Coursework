ANSWER
The overdraft fee is $29 per item when Larkfield pays a check, debit card purchase, or automatic payment that overdraws the account [consumer-fee-schedule.html]. A few conditions affect this:

- No more than three overdraft or returned item fees are charged to one account in a single business day [consumer-fee-schedule.html].
- No fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at the end of the business day [consumer-fee-schedule.html].
- If the member has a linked savings account for overdraft transfers, the transfer used to cover the overdraft is free instead of the $29 fee [consumer-fee-schedule.html].
