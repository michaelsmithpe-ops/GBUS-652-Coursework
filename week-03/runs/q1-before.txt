The overdraft fee is $29 per item paid when Larkfield pays a check, debit card purchase, or automatic payment that overdraws a member's checking account, and the returned item fee is also $29 per item returned [consumer-fee-schedule.html].

A few conditions affect this:

- No overdraft fee is charged if the account is overdrawn by $10 or less at the end of the business day [consumer-fee-schedule.html].
- An overdraft transfer from a linked savings account is free, so no fee applies if the member uses that option [consumer-fee-schedule.html].
