ANSWER
Based on the branch check deposit rules, the first $275 of the day's check deposits is available on the next business day; the remaining amount (here, $1,725) is available on the second business day after the deposit [funds-availability-policy.html].

So of the $2,000 paycheck, the member can use **$275** on the next business day.
