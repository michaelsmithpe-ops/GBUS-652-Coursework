ANSWER
Of the $1,000 check deposited at a branch, the first $275 is available on the next business day; the remaining $725 is available on the second business day after the day of deposit [funds-availability-policy.html].

Note: this assumes the deposit was made before the 5 p.m. cutoff on a business day — if it was made after 5 p.m. or on a weekend/holiday, it is treated as received the next business day, which would shift these availability dates accordingly [funds-availability-policy.html].
