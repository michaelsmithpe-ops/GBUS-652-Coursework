# Segmentation Decision Framework

I don't have context on your product, past campaign data, or business goals, so I can't tell you definitively which segment to prioritize. But here's how to think it through:

## Key Questions to Answer First

1. **What are you launching?** A new plant variety, a tool/accessory, a subscription service? This matters a lot—collectors care about rare specimens, gift buyers care about presentation and ease, beginners care about guidance and low-risk purchases.

2. **What's your goal for the "first" send?**
   - Driving immediate revenue → likely plant collectors (often highest AOV, fastest to convert on something new/rare)
   - Building word-of-mouth/reviews → first-time owners (if the product solves their pain points)
   - Seasonal urgency (holiday, Mother's Day, etc.) → gift buyers, since they're time-sensitive

3. **Do you have engagement data?** Whichever segment has the highest historical open/click rates on launch-type emails is a strong candidate to go first—early engagement often boosts deliverability for subsequent sends.

## A Reasonable Default (if you need to pick now)

**Plant collectors first**, if your product has any novelty, rarity, or "limited" angle—they tend to convert fast and create buzz. Follow with **first-time owners** (nurture/education angle) and **gift buyers** timed closer to relevant occasions.
