# Plant Care Cards

---

### 🌿 Snake Plant (Sansevieria / Dracaena trifasciata)

**Light:** Extremely adaptable — thrives in bright indirect light but tolerates low light well. Avoid direct harsh sun for extended periods (can scorch leaves).

**Water:** Very drought-tolerant. Water every 2–4 weeks, letting soil dry out completely between waterings. Overwatering is the #1 killer — root rot develops quickly in soggy soil.

**Pet Safety:** ⚠️ **Toxic to cats and dogs.** Contains saponins; can cause vomiting, nausea, and drooling if ingested.

---

### 🌿 ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)

**Light:** Tolerates low light but grows best in medium to bright indirect light. Avoid direct sun, which can burn the glossy leaves.

**Water:** Highly drought-tolerant thanks to its rhizomes (store water underground). Water every 2–3 weeks, allowing soil to dry out fully. Very forgiving if you forget to water.

**Pet Safety:** ⚠️ **Toxic to cats and dogs** (and mildly irritating to humans too). Contains calcium oxalate crystals — can cause mouth/throat irritation, vomiting, and skin irritation from sap.

---

### 🌿 Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)

**Light:** Adaptable to low to bright indirect light. Variegated varieties need more light to maintain their patterns; avoid direct sun.

**Water:** Water when the top 1–2 inches of soil are dry (roughly every 1–2 weeks). Likes a thorough soak, then dry-out period. Drooping leaves are a good "water me" signal.

**Pet Safety:** ⚠️ **Toxic to cats and dogs.** Contains calcium oxalate crystals — can cause oral irritation, drooling, and vomiting if chewed/ingested.

---
